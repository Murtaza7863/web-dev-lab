Text and headings

Most of what you put on a web page is text. HTML gives you tags to structure it.

Headings: h1 through h6

There are six heading levels. h1 is the biggest and most important (use once per page, usually).

For your expense tracker, you'd use something like:
<h1>Expense Tracker</h1> at the top,
then maybe <h2>All Expenses</h2> above the list.

Paragraphs: p

Wrap regular sentences in <p> tags. Each paragraph is its own block — the browser adds space between them automatically.

<p>This is one paragraph.</p>
<p>This is another paragraph.</p>

Inline emphasis

These go inside other elements — they don't start a new block:

I spent $45.00 on groceries today.

<p>I spent <strong>$45.00</strong> on <em>groceries</em> today.</p>
  • <strong> — important / bold
  • <em> — emphasis / italic
  • <br> — line break (self-closing, no content, no closing tag needed)

Line breaks

Paragraphs already have space between them. For a break inside a paragraph:

<p>Line one<br>Line two</p>
